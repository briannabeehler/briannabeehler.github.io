Brianna Beehler is a writer and teacher outside of San Francisco. She holds a doctorate in English Literature from the University of Southern California. Her research, writing, and teaching have been supported by the Mellon Foundation, Council for European Studies, and Fulbright Program. Her academic work has appeared in _ELH_ and _Nineteenth-Century Literature_. Her essays and reviews have appeared in _The Georgia Review_, _Public Books_, and elsewhere.
