title: About
---

Brianna Beehler is a writer and teacher outside of San Francisco.
She holds a doctorate in English Literature from the University of Southern California.
Her research, writing, and teaching have been supported by the Mellon Foundation, Council for European Studies, and Fulbright Program.
Her academic work has appeared in *ELH* and *Nineteenth-Century Literature*.
Her essays and reviews have appeared in *The Georgia Review*, *Public Books*, and elsewhere.
